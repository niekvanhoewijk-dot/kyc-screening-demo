Proof of residence

Residential address

One document, issued in the last 3 months

Accepted

  • Utility bill: electricity, gas, water, sewage, internet, TV or home phone
  • Bank or credit card statement. You may hide transactions and amounts, but issuer, your name, address and issue date must stay visible
  • Official government, state or municipal document that states where you live, such as a tax declaration, stay permit or certificate of residence

Not accepted

  • Photos of envelopes or parcels
  • Mobile phone bills
  • P.O. box addresses

A bill in joint names is fine if the other person also completes verification.
