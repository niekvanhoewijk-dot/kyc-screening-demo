Before you start

Three things are needed to open an account. You do not have to finish in one sitting.

  1. 1

    Photo ID and a selfie

    A passport, ID card or driving licence. It must be valid and show all four corners. The selfie confirms the photo is you and is handled by our verification partner.

  2. 2

    Proof of address

    One document issued in the last three months: a utility bill, a bank or credit card statement, or an official government or municipal document that shows where you live.

  3. 3

    A few questions about your finances

    Employment, income, source of funds and what you expect to deposit. We ask these to meet financial regulations.

Opening for a company? You will also need the certificate of incorporation, articles of association, evidence of beneficial ownership, the register of members, and identity documents for every director and shareholder. See the full company list
